Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Khatu Shyam: Darshan & Mandir ("we", "our", or "app") handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a safe devotional experience.

1. Information We Collect

Our app does not require any account creation or registration to use core devotional features. We do not collect personal identifying information like your name, phone number, or home address.

  • Local Device Storage: Your daily Jap counts, streak data, and app preferences (like language selection) are stored completely locally on your device using local storage.
  • Crash & Performance Logs: Standard anonymous diagnostic information may be processed to ensure app stability.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertisements

To support ongoing development and keep the app free for devotees, the app may display advertisements provided by certified third-party ad networks (such as Meta Audience Network, AppLovin, or Google AdMob). These networks may use anonymized advertising IDs to serve relevant ads in compliance with platform policies.

3. Permissions

  • Vibration: Used exclusively to deliver tactile haptic sensations while chanting on the digital Jap mala or ringing temple bells.
  • Internet: Required to fetch daily darshan updates and load devotional media.

4. Children's Privacy

Our application is intended for general audiences of all ages and does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under 13.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@khatushyamapp.com (Apna official email yahan likhein).
